- [Radix Primitives](https://github.com/radix-ui/primitives), MIT.
- [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), ISC.

Model names, release dates and Intelligence Index scores are sourced from public Artificial Analysis pages (model releases and LLM leaderboard). See `src/data/models-source.json` for source URLs, the flagship rule and collection date.
